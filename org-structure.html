Recommendations

Findings from auditing everything — QuickBooks revenue & A/R, the Notion CRM, recurring billing, access hygiene, team economics, and this model itself. Ordered by priority.

01Break the Blair dependency before it breaks youDo now
Audit: Blair is 65.5% of YTD revenue; top two clients are 82%. If Blair pauses, ~two-thirds of cash vanishes in a month. Move: close 2–3 retainers at $900–1,500/mo and push Ekko/podcast (engines 3 & 4) so no client tops 40%. This is the #1 structural fix — the concentration bar turns green only when you widen the base.
02Collect Paulo's overdue $900 todayDo now
Audit: A/R shows Paulo $900 in the 1–30 day bucket; Foresight $2,500 current. Move: fire the ret-media-accountant reminder on Paulo now, and turn on QuickBooks auto-pay for both recurring clients so this never recurs. Set a standing rule: anything past 30 days gets chased automatically.
03Move all account access off your personal GmailDo now
Audit: delegation is blocked because logins live on Garrett's personal Google account — the SMM literally can't be handed the keys safely. Move: stand up a Meta Business Portfolio owned by info@retmediaagency.com, add YouTube managers to the same, and grant task-level (not admin) roles. This unlocks the entire Tier-2 hire.
04Decide Xander by end of August with a number, not a vibeThis month
Audit: Xander is on a free 30-day trial and isn't in QuickBooks — yet Victor's raise to $800–1,000/mo is contingent on it. That's an unfunded liability. Move: set a conversion price now (e.g. rev-share or a monthly floor) and re-eval on a fixed date. If it converts, Victor's raise is funded; if not, hold comp. Don't overhire ahead of the close.
05Bill your owned properties like clientsThis month
Audit: Modern Marketing ($997/mo Premium) and the Ekko 3-tier offer ($799 / $3,497) show $0 in the QuickBooks customer report — owned revenue is invisible. Move: run Modern Marketing seats and every Ekko deliverable through QuickBooks so engines 2–4 show up in the same P&L as retainers. You can't manage concentration you can't see.
06Fund the SMM from new MRR, then hand off publishingThis quarter
Audit: the SMM is the unlock hire but currently $0 (unpaid intern). Your recurring floor is $1,800/mo against a ~$700–1,000 team run-rate — there's room, but it's thin. Move: convert the SMM to paid ($800–1,200) only once one or two new retainers land, then transfer QC + publishing rights so Garrett drops out of the daily pipeline entirely.
07Wire the CRM to the moneyOngoing
Audit: the Notion CRM (New→Won pipeline + Tasks) and the 90-Day Focus scorecard are strong, but disconnected from QuickBooks — "Won" doesn't automatically become an invoice. Move: when a prospect flips to Won, trigger the invoice/recurring setup from ret-media-accountant, and keep the weekly 3-number scorecard (paying clients · guests booked · Ekko sold) as the single accountability layer.
08Keep this model as your live cap-table for the orgOngoing
Audit: comp and revenue now have a single source of truth here. Move: re-run the numbers whenever a rate, client, or deal changes — before you make a hire, check the coverage ratio and net floor guardrails go green. Quarterly, reconcile this against the live QuickBooks report so the defaults never drift from reality.
